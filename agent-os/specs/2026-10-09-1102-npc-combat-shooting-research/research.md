# NPC traffic, retaliation and player shooting reuse
Status: RESEARCH READY. Read-only source investigation; no runtime code, scene or asset modifications.
Source D:/Source/party-craft/Assets; target PA_Craft.
Observed:
1. NPC.prefab contains NPCCombat and AIAction_MoveRandomly script GUIDs. Roaming action samples XZ within range, downward ray against moveArea, asks CharacterNPCMovement.MoveToDestination, selects Walk, completes on arrival/timeout. Prefab range15, height2, timeout10, walkSpeed3/runSpeed6. CharacterNPCMovement delegates path to NavMeshAgent with updatePosition/updateRotation disabled, drives Rigidbody with desiredVelocity, syncs nextPosition, rotates toward travel. This is random destination navigation, not evidence of a fixed road waypoint loop.
2. TrafficPeopleManager caps/spawns NPCs around player on raycast ground and sampled NavMesh, then recycles distant NPC positions toward camera forward. Separate older Traffic_People implementation exists; do not treat it as primary prefab roaming logic. Its Raycast(ray,out hit,walkLayer,5) appears to pass mask/distance in reversed semantic positions.
3. NPCCombat listens CharacterHealth.OnDamageEvent, caches attacker, switches AIManager from Traffic to Combat, subscribes to attacker death and returns to Traffic when attacker dies. BodyAttack and ProjectileAttack chase outside weapon range and attack within range, rate-limited; ranged attack stops movement, sets aim and plays weapon effects. Weapon type/conditions determine response. Source contains null-safety risks for attacker/weapon/target and cancellation lifecycle; do not transplant blindly.
4. Player2.prefab references PlayerInventoryHandler. EquipWeapon maps inventory ID to Opsive ItemSet, starts EquipUnequip; held model and animation are tied to Opsive Item/perspective and animator setup. CharacterPlayer.cs itself is not the shooting implementation.
5. Player weapon prefabs Gun002, Gun004, ToxicGun contain Opsive ShootableWeapon and actual projectile references. Fire uses look-source direction, shot cadence/ammo/fire mode, spawns projectile through pool, initializes damage/owner, then applies effects. Gun002 useRate.6 damage10; ToxicGun.2 damage10; Gun004.1 damage150. Generic ShootableWeapon also supports hitscan but these inspected prefabs use projectile branch.
6. NPC ranged weapon uses Game.Weapon.Projectile separately (pool, barrel spawn, damage10, shooter owner, velocity, lifetime, trigger hit via RecieveDamageObjector to CharacterHealth). Do not confuse this with Opsive player projectile path.
7. PaintBallMissionTracker dispatches FirePaintballs mission progress on enable, not confirmed hit; target mission counter semantics require decision.
Reuse verdict: all three requested behavior slices are extractable by adaptation. NPC state transitions and attack cadence straightforward; navigation and holding/aiming/shooting need target implementation plus explicit animator/hand/muzzle/collider/layer setup. Retain current player/vehicle architecture. Exclude full Opsive stack, UniTask AI framework, inventory shop/profile, analytics, global GameManager/EventDispatcher and unrelated outcome flows.
Target currently has PaintballMissionController unavailable placeholder and no combat/NPC runtime scripts under _Playable_. Unity navigation package installed does not prove Luna Web NavMesh support. Verify current Luna build before choosing NavMesh; limited authored paths plus obstacle checks are an alternative requiring explicit design approval, with different movement semantics. Asset/animation size and browser behavior unmeasured.
