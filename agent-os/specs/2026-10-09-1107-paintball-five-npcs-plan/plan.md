# Paintball mission: five pedestrians and retaliation
## Reviewable behavior
Entering Paintball retains player position and uses existing per-mission camera/player entry settings. Equip Guns_002 visual on a serialized hand socket, enable hold/fire upper-body animation while legs retain walking, show crosshair and right-side Fire hold button. Mouse/touch pointer ownership uses existing DriveHoldButton without driving logic. Hold shoots every0.6sec (source Gun002 baseline, user approved), release/exit/focus loss cancels. Existing joystick/camera controls continue independently.
Aim camera-center ray to choose world point then fire from muzzle to that point. Visible paintball sweeps previous-to-next position each simulation step, so thin obstacles block hits; exclude shooter colliders and stop at first obstruction. Source Gun002 baseline: projectile launch speed40, spread0, one projectile per shot, damage10, magazine12, full-auto/instant. Match source ammo/reload behavior after tracing reload events and reserve initialization; do not assume unlimited ammo or invent reload duration. Range/lifetime and projectile impact behavior must come from referenced [Projectile] Toxic prefab rather than prior arbitrary25m/1sec values. Fixed reusable projectile set size12 avoids repeated allocation. Small impact color effect and existing shot sound if applicable; no new shader.
Exactly5 preplaced NPC instances within approximately6-12m around mission entry street. Each owns assigned walk route graph and home location, random next connected point and idle1-3sec, speed1.5. Ground and capsule probes plus separation avoid walls/overlap; stuck timeout chooses another valid edge. Route points placed on reachable street/sidewalk in actual scene. No global random destination through buildings.
Only struck NPC becomes hostile; other NPCs keep walking. States Walk/Idle/Chase/Attack/Return. Chase speed3, contact range1.4, attack cooldown1sec, one hit per punch after windup with distance/visibility recheck. Chase limited to12m home leash; return home if player escapes. Player receives visual hit feedback and small camera impulse; no health depletion/death/win/lose/endcard in this version. NPC hit gives feedback and aggression, not death. These defaults require approval through this plan.
Opening journal pauses NPC movement/attack and cancels shots; closing restores active mission. Switching away hides gun/Fire/crosshair, restores original player animator, clears projectiles and combat targets. Reselect resets five NPCs to home and idle. Existing vehicle missions remain operational. Paintball receives brief aim/fire hint instead of driving-specific tutorials.
